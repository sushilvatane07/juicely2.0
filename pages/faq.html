Is the juice really made fresh per order?

Yes — every bottle is cold-pressed after you order, not batched in advance or stored overnight.

Do you deliver outside Latur?

How long does the juice stay fresh?

Best consumed within 48 hours, kept refrigerated, since there are no preservatives.

Not yet — today the packs are fixed combinations, but a build-your-own pack is on our roadmap.
